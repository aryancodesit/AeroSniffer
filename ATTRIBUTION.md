## Design & Inspiration

- **DeskBuddy 2.0 hardware platform** — Custom 3D-printed enclosure
  with XIAO ESP32S3 and ST7789 display
- **Vector-style robot face aesthetic** — Inspired by the open-source
  PET desktop robot community
- **Multi-mode architecture** — Inspired by multi-boot gadget concepts
  from the ESP32 maker community
