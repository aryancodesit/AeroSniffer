## Known Limitations

1. **No hardware certification.** Sprint 3 has not undergone hardware validation. All formation rules, dedup windows, decay parameters, and cross-domain behavior are unverified on real hardware.
2. **Salience normalization unvalidated.** The sprint-3 maximum of 55 (SEC_THREAT at 50) is a design estimate. Real-world context multipliers may push values higher.
3. **Ring buffer competition unobserved.** 64 records across 4 domains has not been tested. Touch records may be evicted under heavy security/mood formation.
4. **mood_event subtype `prev_mood` edge case.** When `mood_last_period_type_` resets to `0xFF` on transition, but `observe_mood` is called for MOOD_TRANSITION with `prev_mood=0xFF` — this value is used directly without a guard. May cause confusion in recall if `prev_mood=0xFF` is not handled at read time.
5. **AVI_HIGH_TRAFFIC_WINDOW deferred.** The hardcoded threshold approach was rejected in design review. Sprint 4 needs baseline accumulation for relative traffic comparison.
