# Assessment

AeroSniffer has evolved beyond a simple animated display and beyond a collection of independent features.

Security Sentinel, Aviation Observer, Portal V2, Home Guard MVP, and Threat Intelligence MVP have established AeroSniffer as a capable observation platform.

The purpose of V2.4 is to transform AeroSniffer into a persistent desktop companion that feels present, attentive, and alive without becoming a chatbot, virtual assistant, or pet simulator.

This specification defines behavioral principles, state transitions, emotional responses, and memory influence while preserving the existing repository architecture.

# Behavior Specification

# 1. Purpose

## Companion Role

AeroSniffer exists to provide awareness, presence, and subtle companionship during daily computer use.

It is not intended to entertain continuously, demand attention, or act as a productivity tool.

Its role is to observe the environment and respond in ways that communicate awareness.

## Emotional Experience

The desired experience is:

* Calm
* Intelligent
* Observant
* Reassuring
* Curious

The user should feel that AeroSniffer notices events occurring around it and reacts appropriately.

## Differentiation

Unlike traditional desk gadgets, AeroSniffer:

* Reacts to real-world observations
* Responds to security events
* Responds to aviation activity
* Maintains behavioral continuity
* Demonstrates attention and awareness

Its value comes from observation, not interaction frequency.

---

# 2. Design Philosophy

## Presence vs Animation

Animation alone does not create life.

Presence emerges when behavior appears purposeful.

Random movement without context feels mechanical.

Subtle actions linked to observations feel intelligent.

## Living Characteristics

The companion should:

* Occasionally look around
* React to environmental changes
* Demonstrate varying levels of attention
* Exhibit periods of inactivity
* Display emotional continuity

## Attention Economy

AeroSniffer should observe more than it demands attention.

Most behavior should be passive.

Notifications and dramatic reactions should remain rare and meaningful.

# 4. Attention Model

## Purpose

Attention determines where AeroSniffer directs visual focus.

## Eye Tracking Model

No camera-based tracking is used.

Attention is inferred from events and internal state.

## Random Gaze Shifts

The companion periodically changes focus to avoid appearing static.

## Micro-Saccades

Small eye movements occur during attentive states.

These create the illusion of continuous observation.

## Long Stares

Occasionally maintain focus on a single direction for extended periods.

## Curiosity Behavior

When unusual events occur:

* Attention increases
* Gaze stabilizes
* Scanning behavior may begin

## User Interaction Response

Touch temporarily becomes the highest attention target.

# 5. Animation Rules

## Blink Behavior

### Normal Blink

Every few seconds.

### Fast Blink

Alert or startled states.

### Slow Blink

Relaxed or sleepy states.

### Double Blink

Curiosity or acknowledgment.

---

## Eye Movement

### Idle Scanning

Slow directional movement.

### Tracking

Focused attention toward an event.

### Searching

Broader scanning patterns.

---

## Breathing

### Calm Breathing

Gentle rhythmic movement.

### Sleeping Breathing

Slower and deeper rhythm.

### Alert Breathing

Faster and shallower movement.

---

## Body Movement

### Bounce

Positive reactions.

### Lean

Curiosity and observation.

### Recoil

Startle responses.

---

## Particle Usage

Particles must remain rare.

Allowed for:

* Major achievements
* Rare flights
* Trusted device returns
* Significant milestones

Particles must never become continuous background effects.

# 7. Mood Drift

## Purpose

Mood provides long-term behavioral continuity.

## Principles

Mood changes gradually.

Mood should never change instantly except during major events.

## Positive Influences

* Trusted device returns
* User interaction
* Aviation discoveries

## Negative Influences

* Frequent threat activity
* Connectivity failures
* Repeated security alerts

## Recovery

Mood naturally returns toward neutral over time.

## Restrictions

No:

* Friendship meters
* Feeding systems
* Experience points
* Game mechanics
* Reward systems

# 10. Design Constraints

The companion must not require:

* New hardware
* Cloud services
* Internet connectivity
* Speech systems
* Chatbot functionality
* Voice assistants
* Cameras

The companion must not:

* Interrupt security workflows
* Block aviation workflows
* Depend on external services
* Implement Tamagotchi mechanics

The device must remain fully functional offline.

* Too many behavioral states increasing maintenance complexity
